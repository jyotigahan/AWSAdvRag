MORTGAGE LENDING GUIDELINES — ACME BANK
Effective Date: February 1, 2025
Department: Mortgage Division
Classification: Internal

1. MORTGAGE TYPES OFFERED
ACME Bank offers the following mortgage products: 30-year fixed rate, 15-year fixed rate, 5/1 ARM (Adjustable Rate Mortgage), 7/1 ARM, FHA loans, VA loans, and Jumbo loans. First-time homebuyer programs are available with down payments as low as 3% for conventional loans and 3.5% for FHA loans.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Minimum credit score of 620 for conventional mortgages and 580 for FHA loans. VA loans have no minimum credit score requirement set by the VA, but ACME Bank requires a minimum of 600. Debt-to-income ratio must not exceed 43% for conventional loans and 50% for FHA loans with compensating factors. Minimum employment history of 2 years in the same field is required. Self-employed borrowers must provide 2 years of tax returns showing stable or increasing income.

3. DOWN PAYMENT REQUIREMENTS
Conventional loans require a minimum 5% down payment, or 3% for first-time buyers through the HomeReady program. FHA loans require 3.5% down payment with a credit score of 580 or above, or 10% with scores between 500-579. VA loans offer 0% down payment for eligible veterans. Jumbo loans require a minimum 10% down payment for loan amounts up to $1.5 million and 20% for amounts above $1.5 million.

4. PRIVATE MORTGAGE INSURANCE (PMI)
PMI is required for conventional loans with less than 20% down payment. PMI rates range from 0.3% to 1.5% of the original loan amount annually, depending on credit score and loan-to-value ratio. PMI can be cancelled when the loan-to-value ratio reaches 80% based on the original property value. Automatic PMI termination occurs when the loan-to-value ratio reaches 78%.

5. INTEREST RATES AND FEES
Current 30-year fixed rate: 6.75% APR. Current 15-year fixed rate: 5.99% APR. ARM rates start at 5.25% for the initial fixed period. Origination fee is 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount. Appraisal fee is $450-$650 depending on property type. Title insurance and closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the purchase price.

6. PROPERTY REQUIREMENTS
Property must be the borrower's primary residence, second home, or investment property. Investment properties require an additional 5% down payment above standard requirements. Property appraisal must be conducted by a bank-approved appraiser. Flood insurance is required for properties in FEMA-designated flood zones. Homeowner's insurance must be maintained for the life of the loan with ACME Bank listed as the loss payee.

7. LOAN PROCESSING TIMELINE
Pre-approval: 1-3 business days. Full application to conditional approval: 7-14 business days. Conditional approval to clear-to-close: 5-10 business days. Closing: typically 30-45 days from application. Rate lock available for 30, 45, or 60 days.
