CREDIT CARD TERMS AND CONDITIONS — ACME BANK
Effective Date: April 1, 2025
Department: Cards Division
Classification: Public

1. CARD TYPES AND ANNUAL FEES
ACME Classic Card: No annual fee, credit limit $1,000-$10,000. ACME Gold Card: $95 annual fee (waived first year), credit limit $5,000-$25,000. ACME Platinum Card: $195 annual fee, credit limit $10,000-$50,000. ACME Business Card: $150 annual fee, credit limit $10,000-$100,000.

2. INTEREST RATES
Purchase APR: 16.99% to 24.99% variable, based on creditworthiness. Balance transfer APR: 0% introductory rate for 15 months, then 16.99% to 24.99%. Cash advance APR: 26.99% with no grace period. Penalty APR: 29.99% applied after 60 days of delinquency, reviewed after 6 months of on-time payments.

3. REWARDS PROGRAM
ACME Classic: 1% cash back on all purchases. ACME Gold: 2% cash back on dining and groceries, 1% on everything else. ACME Platinum: 3% on travel and dining, 2% on groceries, 1% on everything else, plus airport lounge access. Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Minimum redemption threshold is 2,500 points ($25 value).

4. FEES
Late payment fee: $29 for the first occurrence, $39 for subsequent occurrences within 6 billing cycles. Returned payment fee: $29. Foreign transaction fee: 3% for Classic, 0% for Gold and Platinum. Cash advance fee: 5% of the amount or $10, whichever is greater. Balance transfer fee: 3% of the amount or $5, whichever is greater.

5. BILLING AND PAYMENTS
Minimum payment is the greater of $25 or 2% of the outstanding balance plus any fees and interest. Grace period of 25 days from the statement closing date for purchases. No grace period for cash advances — interest accrues from the transaction date. Payments are applied first to minimum payment, then to highest APR balances.

6. CREDIT LIMIT MANAGEMENT
Automatic credit limit increase reviews are conducted every 6 months for accounts in good standing. Customers may request a credit limit increase through online banking or by calling customer service. Over-limit transactions are declined by default. Customers may opt-in to over-limit coverage with a fee of $35 per billing cycle.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Billing disputes must be filed within 60 days of the statement date. The bank will investigate and provide a resolution within 30 days. Provisional credit is issued within 10 business days for disputed amounts exceeding $50. Chargeback rights are governed by Visa/Mastercard network rules.
